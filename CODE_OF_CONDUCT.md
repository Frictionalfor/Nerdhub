# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NerdHub is built around curiosity. We want people of different backgrounds, experience levels and disciplines to participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not a person's worth.
- Welcome genuine beginner questions.
- Give credit and cite sources.
- Respect privacy, boundaries and different perspectives.
- Disagree without harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Report harmful behavior rather than escalating it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination or targeted abuse.
- Threats, stalking, doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content.
- Spam, scams, impersonation or coordinated manipulation.
- Malicious links, malware, credential theft or platform abuse.
- Plagiarism, deceptive attribution or knowingly false reports.
- Retaliation against people who report concerns.

## Moderation

Moderators may remove content, restrict features, issue warnings, suspend accounts or take other proportionate action. Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent removal. Moderation decisions should be documented and appealable where practical.

## Reporting

Use the platform's report controls when available. Until then, contact the maintainers through the repository's published security or community contact channel. Do not post private evidence publicly.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, community spaces and official NerdHub events.

## Enforcement

Maintainers should act consistently, proportionately and with due regard for privacy. They should document significant actions, manage conflicts of interest and provide an appeal path where appropriate.
